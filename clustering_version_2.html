Formulas

1 · Assign
zi = argminj ‖xi − cj‖

Each point joins its nearest centroid.

2 · Update
cj = (1 / |Sj|) · Σ x  for x in Sj

The centroid is the average of its cluster's points.

Cost (elbow chart)
avg dist = (1 / n) · Σ ‖xi − czi‖

Mean distance from each point to its own centroid.

1 · Closest pair
(A, B) = argmin ‖cA − cB‖

Compare the centroids of every pair of clusters.

2 · Merge
cAB = (|A|·cA + |B|·cB) / (|A| + |B|)

Same as the average of all points in the merged cluster.
