Clustering Lab is published. It has k-means and hierarchical clustering on fake blob-plus-outlier data, a method toggle and a "New data" button. I haven't opened it in a browser, so layout and click behavior are untested. I only syntax-checked the script and ran the clustering math in Node.

- **k-means:**
  - Step alternates between assigning points and moving centroids.
  - Play/Pause, Reset and a k slider are there.
  - Click the plot to place starting centroids.
  - "Bad init" picks the worst of 80 random bunched starts, so it usually gets stuck. A start that is bad for one dataset can still recover on another, so try a few datasets.
  - After it converges, it reports how far its cost is above the best found for that k.
- **Elbow chart:** average distance to centroid for k=1 to 8, using the best of 10 runs per k. The detected elbow is highlighted and your current run is marked, so you can compare them.
- **Hierarchical:** each step merges the closest pair of centroids. The merged cluster shows its centroid, and the dendrogram draws as you go. A slider lets you scrub back and forth through the merges.
- **Formulas:** assign, centroid average and the cost formula for k-means; closest pair and the weighted centroid merge for hierarchical. The formula that just ran is highlighted yellow, along with changed points, cluster counts and centroid coordinates, plus a worked number example each step.

One caveat: the outliers keep the elbow from being perfectly sharp. The curve flattens past the true k but never fully.
